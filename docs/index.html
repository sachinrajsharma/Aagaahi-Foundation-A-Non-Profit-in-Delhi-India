Our work, in pictures

Highlights from programmes and events we have run in the community.

Skill Development & Women Empowerment

Hands-on training that builds livelihoods and safer workplaces for women.

Hunar Ki Dor

Craft and tailoring workshops where women learn marketable skills and turn everyday materials into products.

POSH Training

Training sessions on the Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the workplace for professionals and teams.

Healthy Hygiene & Wellbeing

Taking health, hygiene and safety awareness to the people who need it, where they live and work.

Health Camp

Health check-up camps that bring screening and care closer to the community.

Kids Hygiene

Hygiene awareness sessions and kits for school children.

Menstrual Hygiene

Open conversations on menstrual health with women and girls in the community.

Road Safety

Street-play road-safety drives in Delhi markets, held with traffic police and covered by the press.

Financial Literacy & Cyber Security

Helping people manage money wisely and stay safe online.

Financial Literacy & Cyber Security

Awareness sessions for community members and police personnel on safe money habits and online security.
